## SuperDocs

**AI document editing. Inside the document.**

SuperDocs is an AI document editing platform — the AI works inside your document, not in a sidebar or chat window. Describe what you want changed in plain English, and the AI edits your document directly. No copy-paste. Formatting stays intact.

### Available three ways

- **Free web app** — try it at [use.superdocs.app](https://use.superdocs.app)
- **Server-side API** — integrate AI document editing into your product in under an hour, any programming language
- **MCP server** — your AI agents edit documents autonomously

### Built for three audiences

- People who want AI to edit their documents without the copy-paste workflow
- Developers who want to add AI document editing to their products via API
- AI agents that connect directly via MCP — no human in the loop required
